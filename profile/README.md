## How a case works

A sovereign client asks: *"should we be worried about Institution X?"*

The substrate runs the question through the case pipeline. ATLAS pulls the public corpus on the institution. SCRUTATOR runs cross-civilisational research against the question. COHORS reads the institution's behaviour through twenty-two civilisational dialects to surface convergent patterns. ARETE certifies the case against the substrate's accumulated calibration history. SEPULCRUM seals every artefact with a SHA-256 chain anchor and a Ghost Network RFC 3161 timestamp.

The customer receives, sub-hour:

- The institution's genome — which of the 43 institutional genes are active, at what intensity, with what supporting evidence
- The fragility score — calibrated probability of collapse on each of five horizon bands (nowcast, tactical, operational, strategic, civilisational)
- The collapse pathway — which of the 15 frameworks are firing, in what order, with what tipping points
- The evidence chain — every claim sourced, every source sealed, every seal verifiable offline
- The calibration history — Auryum's track record on similar cases, with Brier-grade scoring

Cases stay alive after delivery. ARGUS picks up that the subject was arrested last week. SCRUTATOR's corpus catches a new disclosure filing. The case updates in place. The customer asks once and stays informed.

The case library currently contains forty-four certified institutional cases. Subjects include FTX, Wirecard, and other public institutional failures — runs that produced both empirical validation of the framework set and live operational cases. The methodology is documented at [methodology/CASES.md](https://github.com/Auryum-Systems/.github/blob/main/methodology/CASES.md).
