## Why most of this is private

Auryum operates a three-tier classification model. Public claims sit at this profile and at the website. Client-facing materials are released under NDA during sovereign engagements. Internal substrate (ontology, lexicons, codec atoms, simulation templates, calibration history) stays in vault — AWS S3 with KMS encryption — never in tracked source.

The seven repositories backing this organisation are private because the institutional-fragility substrate is the trade-secret content that gives the products their edge. Architectures are patentable. Lexicons are not. Both stay protected.
