# Auryum

**Institutional fragility intelligence.**

Auryum reads how institutions fail, and how close they are to failing, before the failure is visible from the inside.

Most analysis of institutional failure is written after the event. Auryum is built to read the pattern in advance: to map the structural and behavioural dynamics that run ahead of collapse, score how fragile an institution is, and show the evidence behind every claim.

## What we are building

Auryum is an AI research lab and the substrate beneath it: a memory, a language, a simulation engine and a cryptographic proof layer that together read institutional fragility and keep the reasoning auditable over time.

A small number of focused products sit on that substrate, each pointing the same capability at a different problem:

- **VERUM** — a fragility intelligence API.
- **ORBIS** — institutional simulation: culturally embodied agents run an institution forward until its failure emerges, never scripted.
- **SONUS** — synthetic-media and identity protection for people who have become targets.
- **MNEME** — the memory substrate the rest is built on.

## The approach

Three thousand years of institutional and civilisational collapse, read systematically and reduced to a typed body of patterns: 102 genes, 15 fragility frameworks, 3 named compounds and 46 genomes, 166 ontology entities in all. The specific definitions are confidential and held under encryption, never in public source. Every claim the system makes is sealed with cryptographic provenance, so it can be verified long after it is written.

## Why most of this is private

The pattern library is the trade secret, so the repositories that hold it are private by design. This profile and the website are the public face. Anything substantive sits behind a signed agreement.
