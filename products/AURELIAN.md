# AURELIAN

**The post-detection child-protection response layer.**

When AI-generated harmful content depicting a child is found, what happens next?

Existing detection products produce alerts and dashboards. They do not produce a trained welfare contact who has been through a two-day course modelled on NHS Bereavement Service training. They do not produce CPR Part 35-grade evidence files for criminal proceedings. They do not produce the photography-partnership consent model that turns parental biometric consent — the highest-friction problem in child-safety technology — into a single tick-box at the moment a school photo is taken.

## The gap

Detection is well-funded. Government grant programmes worldwide are running races for bigger classifiers, faster scanners, broader corpora. Detection vendors compete on detection metrics.

Everything that happens after a match is found is unowned.

- Who calls the parent
- How they say it
- What the parent does next
- Where the evidence goes so a prosecution sticks
- How the child's biometric is protected during the entire process

AURELIAN is the response layer detection vendors do not build.

## How it works

School photographs are converted into protective biometric embeddings under a partnership-driven consent model — the partnership lives between AURELIAN and the photography company, so the parent gives consent once, at the moment of the school photo, not in fifteen separate dialogue boxes across fifteen platforms.

Embeddings are monitored against AI-generated content for matches. Match triggers a humane evidence-grade response. Trained welfare contact. Parental notification protocol with bereavement-service-grade tone. Evidence chain to the National Crime Agency. Cryptographic chain of custody throughout, so the file the NCA receives passes evidential scrutiny months or years later.

## The naming

Previously named SAFEGUARD. Renamed AURELIAN — from the Latin *aurelia*, golden — protecting the child, not the platform.

## Status

Pitch-ready. Fifteen operational specs. Not yet built. Awaiting government grant funding plus signed photography-partner agreement to enter the build phase. Target launch: 2027.

The reason AURELIAN is not under construction yet is that the architecture insists on the photography-partnership consent model and the trained welfare contact protocol. Both require partner agreements that exceed what a solo founder ships unilaterally. The product is genuinely ready for the partner; the partner is the bottleneck, not the build.
