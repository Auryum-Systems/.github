# SONUS

**Synthetic-identity protection for high-value individuals.**

In Q1 of 2025, AI-generated content targeting athletes increased 81 percent quarter on quarter. Fabricated endorsements. Deepfaked intimate content. False statements attributed in synthetic video. Platform moderation catches under 20 percent before viral spread.

The cost lands on the individual: lost endorsement revenue, reputational injury, frequent legal exposure, occasional safety threat.

## What SONUS does

Detection runs forensic-grade against the client's authenticated likeness. Five-level authenticity scale, two-analyst hard rule, confidence ratings on every classification.

Commercial loss quantification uses the CLF (Commercial Loss Framework) methodology. Not *"this looks bad"* but *"this is a Level 4 incident, our CLF assessment estimates £X to £Y commercial impact across these five vectors with stated assumptions, and here is the calibration history of similar incidents."*

Evidence files are hash-chained, eIDAS-signed, and pass CPR Part 35 standards for civil proceedings. The athlete's lawyer can take the file directly to court.

## Why now

Three forces converging in 2026 created the gap SONUS fills.

EU AI Act Article 50, enforceable from 2 August 2026, requires machine-readable labelling on synthetic media. Its absence becomes a documented regulatory breach.

UK Personality Rights Bill, in committee 2026–27, creates a statutory cause of action for unauthorised likeness use.

US Image Rights Coalition reform, April 2027, strengthens commercial synthetic content claims with federal pre-emption of state-by-state inconsistency.

Existing competitors do monitoring dashboards (no legal grade) or reputational risk reports (no forensic methodology). Nobody combines all three.

## Delivery

A Monthly Intelligence Brief that arrives every month, even when there are zero incidents. The protection is in the consistency of being watched, not in the urgency of an alert.
