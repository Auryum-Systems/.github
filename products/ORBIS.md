# ORBIS

**The institutional twin and emergent drama engine.**

ORBIS runs autonomous AI agents inside institutional pressure and watches what comes out. The agents have eleven layers of embodied psychology: genome, cultural inheritance, moral commitments, memory, dreams, sensory anchors, temporal loops, economic state. They are not characters in a script. They are people in a building.

## How it produces emergence rather than prompted output

The architecture enforces a double wall between the analytical layer and the simulation layer. No analytical vocabulary enters the agents' prompts. No observation findings enter the simulation state. The wall is what makes the output scientifically valid. Without it, a multi-agent system produces stylised completions of the prompt's framing. With it, the system produces drama that the analyst did not anticipate.

## The first full run

The Corporation. Fifty rounds, fifty-five agents, modelled after a 2008-era investment bank under crisis. Total compute cost: $4.38 on Sonnet via Batch API.

Output included Richard Calder's full character arc — including the unscripted moment where he confronted leadership with *"you are calling the chicken a duck."* A cognitive-dissonance call-out that emerged from his genome plus cultural inheritance plus accumulated round-by-round observation, with no prompt asking him to perform the role of moral-courage figure.

Eight of fifteen institutional fragility frameworks were empirically validated by the simulation. Two new cross-cultural convergence patterns surfaced as glyph candidates for the substrate's discovered ontology. Four agents departed the institution emergently when their moral injury exceeded their economic dependency.

## Three product lines on the same engine

**Studio.** Premium emergent drama for streaming. Three format innovations: Girard Subtitles (three-layer character cognition rendered visible on screen — what they believe, what they say, what they desire), interactive emergent forking (each viewer fork produces genuinely independent simulated paths, not the pre-written choose-your-own-adventure trees of *Bandersnatch* or *Telltale*), and persistent characters across seasons. Five-show slate: Mars, Rome, Boardroom, School, Hospital.

**Mirror.** The institutional twin product. A client institution buys a simulation seeded with their actual genome (anonymised). They watch their own institutional dynamics play out under shocks they specify. They see the cascade nobody talks about. The closest analogue is a wind-tunnel test for organisations.

**Training.** Insert Mode for high-stakes scenarios. Executive teams, military command, medical residents, family-office heirs are inserted into a simulation as a character. Five formats from light awareness sessions to multi-day immersive wargames with five humans against forty-five AI agents. Fundamentally different from a tabletop exercise: the simulation responds to participants' actual decisions with consequences nobody pre-wrote.
