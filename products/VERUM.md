# VERUM

**Fragility intelligence as an API.**

A sovereign client (bank, hedge fund, defence prime, family office, regulator) asks: *"should we be worried about Institution X?"* VERUM runs the question through Auryum's substrate and returns a certified intelligence case in under an hour. Genome, fragility score, collapse pathway, evidence chain, calibration history. Cryptographically signed so the customer can verify it without trusting Auryum.

## What VERUM sits between

Strategy intelligence today has two failure modes.

Forrester or McKinsey research reports take weeks to produce. Cost five-figure sums each. Static the moment delivered. Stale within a month. No mechanism to update when reality moves.

AI chatbot answers are sub-second. Effectively free. No provenance, no calibration, nothing a regulator or board would accept in defence of a decision.

VERUM is the institutional middle.

## Three properties competitors do not combine

**Calibrated.** Tetlock-grade probability with horizon-specific calibration history attached to every claim. The case shows its track record on similar questions, not just its current best guess.

**Provenance-defensible.** Every evidence chain sealed and cryptographically anchored. The customer verifies offline, with no Auryum cooperation required. A regulator querying the case in three years gets the same proof Auryum delivered today.

**Live, not static.** The case stays alive after delivery. When public corpus catches a new disclosure filing, when ARGUS picks up that the subject was arrested last week, the case updates in place. You ask once. You stay informed forever.

## Delivery model

Infrastructure embedded in customer workflows, not a destination dashboard. The Stripe pattern: API plus email plus Slack plus Outlook plus iManage plus a thin audit dashboard for compliance. The customer never logs in to a separate Auryum portal to read the case. The case meets them where they already are.
