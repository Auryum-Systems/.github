# SUBSTRATE

**The institutional ontology and the multi-agent reading engine.**

The substrate is what makes Auryum's six products possible. Without it, MNEME is a memory layer with nothing to remember about. ORBIS is a simulation with no agent psychology. VERUM is an API with no certified case to return. STELE is a codec with no semantic content to encode. The substrate is the engine. The products are the surfaces.

## What the substrate is

A typed institutional ontology, accumulated and validated over three thousand years of recorded civilisational collapse. The ontology is built bottom-up from cross-civilisational reading, not top-down from theoretical taxonomy.

The specific identifiers — gene IDs, framework names, compound names, genome profile identifiers, activation rules, decay constants — are trade-secret content that lives in AWS S3 with KMS encryption, not in tracked source. The Auryum codebase carries vault-key references; the substance is resolved at runtime.

## How patterns get into the ontology

Three independent discovery streams. A pattern witnessed in only one stream is a hypothesis. A pattern witnessed in two streams is a finding. A pattern witnessed in all three streams is a law candidate.

**Cross-civilisational reading.** COHORS (the cohort of cohorts) runs twenty-two civilisational dialects against the same source corpus. When a Russian cohort reads imperial collapse and the Mandarin cohort reads dynastic succession crisis and the Arabic cohort reads waqf institutional dissolution and all three independently surface the same gene, the gene is corroborated across civilisations.

**Production cases.** VERUM cases on real institutional subjects (FTX, Wirecard, twenty-first-century banking failures) provide contemporary evidence. When the same gene fires in a 2026 case as in a 1789 case, the gene is empirically alive.

**Embodied simulation.** ORBIS runs autonomous AI agents inside institutional pressure. Patterns that emerge in simulation without being prompted, validated against the cross-civilisational and production-case evidence, become the strongest possible class of finding — predicted by structure, observed in history, replicated under controlled emergent conditions.

The first full ORBIS simulation (the Corporation, fifty rounds, fifty-five agents, $4.38) empirically validated eight of the fifteen frameworks and produced two new compound candidates that VERTEBRUS — the substrate's ontology custodian — has under panel review.

It is twenty thousand pages of cross-civilisational primary research compressed into typed atoms, with calibration history accumulated case by case, with cohort dialects trained on specific civilisational corpora, with simulation runs that empirically test the activation conditions.

Every product Auryum ships compounds on the substrate. Every case adds to the calibration history. Every simulation run adds emergent evidence. Every cohort dialect refinement improves cross-civilisational corroboration. The substrate is the moat that gets deeper with deployment, not the moat that gets shallower.

A competitor entering this space has to either acquire three thousand years of typed institutional reading at cohort scale, or buy the substrate. There is no third option that produces calibrated, provenance-defensible fragility intelligence.
