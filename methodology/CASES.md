# Case Methodology

How a certified Auryum case is produced, sealed, and kept alive.

## What a case is

A sovereign client asks a question about an institution. *Should we be worried about X? What is its current fragility profile? What does its likely collapse pathway look like over the next six months?*

The substrate runs the question through a typed pipeline. The output is a certified intelligence case — sub-hour, calibrated, provenance-defensible, kept alive after delivery.

The case is not a research report. It is a structured artefact that combines the institution's genome, its current fragility score on five horizon bands, its likely collapse pathway through the framework library, the evidence chain that supports every claim, and the calibration history that defines Auryum's track record on similar questions.

## The pipeline

Six commanders run the case in sequence. Each commander has one task and reports to the next.

**ATLAS.** Surfaces the public corpus on the subject institution. Filings, news, analyst reports, regulatory disclosures, court records. Ranks by relevance to the question.

**SCRUTATOR.** Runs research against the question across the substrate's reading registers. Pulls historical analogues. Identifies which of the 43 institutional genes are likely to be active.

**COHORS.** Reads the institution's recent behaviour through twenty-two civilisational dialects. The Russian cohort reads it through Romanov-collapse priors. The Mandarin cohort reads it through dynastic-succession priors. The Arabic cohort reads it through waqf-dissolution priors. Convergent patterns across cohorts strengthen claims; divergent patterns flag uncertainty.

**ARETE.** Certifies the case against the substrate's accumulated calibration history. ARETE refuses to certify a case where confidence is below tier-floor minimums or where evidence is below tri-witness threshold.

**ATLAS (second pass).** Cross-pattern matches the certified case against the existing case library to surface analogues — *"this case looks like a Stage 3 of the Wirecard pattern combined with a Stage 1 of the FTX pathway."*

**SEPULCRUM.** Seals the case. SHA-256 chain anchor. Ghost Network RFC 3161 timestamp. The seal can be verified offline in three years without Auryum's cooperation.

## What the customer receives

A structured response with seven components.

The institution's **genome**: which of the 43 institutional genes are active, at what intensity, with what supporting evidence per gene.

The institution's **fragility score**: calibrated probability of collapse on each of five horizon bands (nowcast, tactical, operational, strategic, civilisational). Each probability carries a calibration history showing Auryum's track record on similar questions.

The **collapse pathway**: which of the 15 frameworks are firing, in what order, with what gene-level tipping points. Specifically *"if Gene-X-equivalent activates above intensity 0.7 within the next quarter, Framework-Y-equivalent firing becomes >0.85 likely within the following two quarters."*

The **evidence chain**: every claim sourced, every source SEPULCRUM-sealed, every seal verifiable offline.

The **calibration history**: Auryum's track record on cases that share the genome shape, expressed as Brier-grade scoring on resolved historical predictions.

The **counterfactual frame**: what the case would look like if specified genes were not active. This is the actionable intelligence — the customer can ask *"what would change if intervention X was made?"*

The **subscription handle**: the case stays alive. ARGUS picks up new public information. SCRUTATOR's corpus catches new filings. The case updates in place. The customer asks once and stays informed.

## What sealed provenance buys the customer

A sovereign client decision-maker (board chair, regulator, fund principal, defence procurement officer) cannot use intelligence they cannot defend. *"Where did this come from? Who certified it? Can we verify it three years from now if there is litigation?"*

Sealed provenance answers all three.

Every artefact in the case carries a chain-walk-verifiable seal back to its source. The Ghost Network RFC 3161 timestamp anchors the seal in time independently of Auryum — the customer can verify the case existed in this exact form on this exact date, without trusting Auryum to confirm it.

This is what differentiates Auryum from chatbot research and from research-firm reports. A regulator querying the case in 2030 receives the same proof Auryum delivered today.

## The case library

The substrate's case library currently contains forty-four certified institutional cases. The library serves three purposes.

It is the calibration corpus — every new case is scored against the historical accuracy of similar cases.

It is the analogue database — every new case is cross-pattern-matched against the library to surface "this looks like X plus Y" framings.

It is the empirical evidence base — the library demonstrates which frameworks fire under which conditions, refining the substrate's activation rules and decay constants over time.

Subjects in the library include public institutional failures whose post-mortems are matters of public record (FTX, Wirecard, several twenty-first-century banking failures) plus contemporary cases on institutions of active interest. Specific subject identifiers and case content are CLIENT-tier — released under NDA during sovereign engagement — except where a case has been published as a public methodological example.
